# Code of conduct

Kalshi Bot's issues and discussions are open to everyone. To keep them useful and welcoming, everyone taking part agrees to the following.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent, including when you disagree.
- Keep to the topic: bugs, features and how Kalshi Bot works.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including account details, balances or positions.
- Promoting trading services, signals or schemes, or presenting predictions as financial advice.
- Spam, or repeatedly posting the same request.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block participants who don't follow this code. To report a problem privately, use the repository's [private reporting](https://github.com/jaldrich2023/KalshiBot/security/advisories/new) form and say it's a conduct report; it will be reviewed and kept confidential.

This code is adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
