Inside Kalshi Bot

Everything a 15-minute trader needs, on one screen

The settlement average, live

The same 60-second average Kalshi settles on, with the target, the window's actual high and low and the odds of breaking them, the trend and projected close, and your exits. Every layer can be switched on or off.

A log that explains itself

Every call, its confidence, Kalshi's price and bid/ask, and the result. Rest the pointer on a row for a plain-language breakdown: where the price stood, whether the lead held, and how close it finished.

Performance by day, hour, week and month

The best times to trade, ranked; hour-by-hour and day × hour heatmaps; and the cumulative edge over Kalshi, with emerging trends flagged as early signs or confirmed.

Indicators that earn their weight

Each signal shows its value at the call and now, its weight, and why it is or isn't moving the call. Signals are re-tested after every window, and none counts until it beats the market on windows it hasn't seen.

Calibrated, and proven on unseen windows

When Kalshi Bot says 70%, the called side wins about 70% of the time. Every signal has to improve predictions on windows it wasn't trained on before it counts, so the bot anchors on Kalshi's well-calibrated odds and adds only what's proven, automatically, after every window. At a glance

  • Calls any minute from +3 to +12; optional confident-calls-only mode
  • Ten exchange feeds, each calibrated to Kalshi's official targets
  • Calibrated reversal risk, momentum strength and breakout odds
  • Custom stop-loss (5–70%) and take-profit (5–100%); trailing stop
  • Trains on up to 180 days of history and retrains after every close
  • Never places orders; your log and settings stay on your Mac
